ForeverData - where its data comes from, and under which terms
==============================================================

ForeverData's code (Core.lua) and its data files (Data/*.lua) are under the
GNU General Public License v3.0 (LICENSE.txt), with one added term from
ForeverDB.net's data (GPL-3.0 section 7(b)): keep the notices in the files,
and credit "Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)" where a work
that reuses this data lists its credits and sources.

The data files are generated by `forever addon` (wow-forever-data, the
author's offline database) from these sources:

- ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net): what ForeverDB players' addons
  recorded in WoW Forever - boss loot counted over looted kills, rares'
  spots, levels and loot, vendors and trainers where they stand, Merchant's
  Favor offers and their costs, Forever's quests and who starts and ends
  them. Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), GPL-3.0 with the
  attribution term above. Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net.
- CMaNGOS classic-db (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db): Classic 1.12's
  loot tables, spawns, vendors and trainers, where nothing better is known.
  Copyright (C) the CMaNGOS project and its contributors, GPL-3.0.
- wowhead (https://www.wowhead.com/forever): drop, skinning, pickpocketing,
  gathering and fishing counts in WoW Forever and Classic Era, which items
  are available to players, spawn spots, vendor stock and reputation, and
  trainer costs.
- QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB): spawns, drops and
  quests. GPL-3.0.
- The WoW Forever client: names and numbers from the game's own tables.
  World of Warcraft, WoW Forever and their names are trademarks or property
  of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. ForeverData is not affiliated with or
  endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
